Leagues

The five Central Asian football federations, country by country: Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan. All five were founded in 1992, after the dissolution of the USSR, and today compete under different confederations — Kazakhstan in UEFA, the other four in the AFC — with very different levels of professionalisation, budget and international standing.

We haven't identified a single active Spanish player in any of the five leagues yet. It's still a market waiting to be discovered.
