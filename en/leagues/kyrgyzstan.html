Context

The Kyrgyz Premier League is Kyrgyzstan's top football division, played since 1992 under the Football Federation of the Kyrgyz Republic, within the AFC. Like Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan, the league expanded from 14 to 16 teams this 2026 season — an expansion shared by three of the five countries in the region this same year.

Reigning champions are Bars Issyk-Kul, who won their first-ever title in 2025, though they currently sit 4th. The league's most successful club historically, Dordoi Bishkek (13 titles), is having a quiet mid-table season. Current leaders are Alga Bishkek.

The league champion enters the AFC Challenge League, the continental competition for lower-ranked AFC federations.

As of this update we haven't identified any Spanish player currently active in this league.
