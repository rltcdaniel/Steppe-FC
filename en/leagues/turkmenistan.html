Context

The Ýokary Liga is Turkmenistan's top football division, played since 1992 and run by the Football Federation of Turkmenistan within the AFC. It's the smallest of the five leagues: just 8 clubs, who face each other up to four times over a 28-round season (April to November).

Reigning champions FC Arkadag are having a historic season: unbeaten through the first 17 rounds (17 wins, 0 draws, 0 losses), chasing a fourth straight title. The club, based in the newly-built city of Arkadag (opened in 2023), is the dominant force in Turkmen football right now, though the league's all-time most successful club remains Altyn Asyr, with 8 titles.

The champion enters the AFC Champions League Two preliminary stage; if they also win the Turkmenistan Cup, the AFC Challenge League spot passes to the league runner-up instead.

As of this update we haven't identified any Spanish player currently active in this league.
