Context

The Tajikistan Higher League (known by sponsorship as the Parimatch Higher League) is Tajikistan's top football division, played since 1992 within the AFC. 12 teams take part this season.

Istiqlol, of Dushanbe, are the absolute dominant force in this league's history: 14 titles, reigning champions, and top of the table again this season. No other club comes close in honours.

The champion enters the AFC Champions League Two. Among the top scorers there's a notable African contingent (Ghana, Cameroon) plus an Uzbek player — the Tajik league appears relatively open to a diverse mix of foreign signings.

As of this update we haven't identified any Spanish player currently active in this league.
