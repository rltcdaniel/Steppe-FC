Context

The Kazakhstan Premier League is the country's top football division, played since 1992. Unlike the rest of Central Asia, Kazakhstan has competed in UEFA competitions since 2002 (previously AFC), making it the region's league with the most genuine European exposure.

The 2026 season expanded from 14 to 16 teams, with three clubs promoted from the First Division (Kaspiy, Altai and Irtysh Pavlodar) and only one relegation in this transition season.

Reigning champions Kairat Almaty pulled off the biggest shock of the 2025-26 Champions League qualifying rounds: they eliminated Celtic (0-0 in Glasgow, 0-0 in Almaty, 3-2 on penalties) on 26 August 2025, becoming the easternmost club ever to reach the Champions League league phase — only the second Kazakh club in history to play in the competition, after Astana in 2015-16.

The league champion enters the Champions League qualifying rounds; the next places give access to the Conference League. The league is broadcast domestically on Qazsport, and internationally on Eleven Sports and OneFootball.

As of this update we haven't identified any Spanish player currently active in this league — a still-untapped market for Spanish talent.
