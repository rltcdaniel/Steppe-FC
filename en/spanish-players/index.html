Spanish Players

We've found seven Spanish footballers currently active in Central Asia's leagues, split between Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan — plus two coaches. In Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan and Tajikistan, for now, there are none. Click each card to see who they are, where they've played, and where they are today.

Spanish dugouts

Beyond the players, there's Spanish coaching presence in the region too. Catalan coach Francesc Bonet manages Bars, Kyrgyzstan's reigning champions and Albert Torras's club. In Tajikistan, Andalusian coach Juan Cortés started the season in charge of CSKA Dushanbe, though we haven't been able to confirm whether he's still in the role as of this update.

Where there's no Spanish presence yet

Uzbekistan: no Spanish player is currently active in the Super League, though there was one until recently: forward Rubén Sánchez played for Surkhon Termez and AGMK between 2022 and 2025, before moving on to the Thai league.

Tajikistan: no Spanish player in its league, which Istiqlol dominate almost single-handedly. There has, as noted above, been Spanish presence on the bench.

Turkmenistan: no Spanish player or coach at all. The country's closed nature and political particularities make it hard for foreign professionals of any origin to arrive.

Sources: Wikipedia (player profiles), Migrantes del Balón (a specialist outlet tracking Spanish footballers abroad), LinkedIn and Soccerway. Verified as of this update — the transfer market can change this information at any time.
