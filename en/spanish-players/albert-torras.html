Biography

Albert Torras Crespo played for Penya Barcelonista Sant Celoni, RCD Espanyol and, from 2011, FC Barcelona's La Masia. In 2013, aged just 17, he moved to England to sign for Wolverhampton Wanderers, then in the Championship.

Back in Spain he built a well-travelled career across regional football and Spain's fourth tier: Málaga, Zaragoza (making his first-team debut in 2019), Ejea, Lleida, Calahorra, Badalona Futur and Olot. In 2025 he tried his luck in India, with Gokulam Kerala in the I-League, without making a first-team appearance, and in February 2026 he signed for Bars, Kyrgyzstan's reigning champions.

He is, along with Palma-born Munir Abdeselam and fellow Mallorcan Ángel Sánchez Baró, one of only three Spanish footballers to have ever played in the Kyrgyz league — and the only Catalan of the three.
