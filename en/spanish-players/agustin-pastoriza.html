Biography

Agustín Emanuel Pastoriza Cacabelos, known as "Pibe", was born in Argentina but moved with his family to Galicia at just five years old, to Cambados, his mother's hometown. His entire football education is Spanish: he came through Pontevedra and made his La Liga debut with Real Betis in 2014, aged just 18.

His adult career has played out almost entirely in Galician regional football (Pontevedra, Ourense, Arenteiro) and Spain's fourth tier (Cultural Leonesa), with a brief spell in Finland along the way. In January 2026 he signed for Kazakhstan's Aktobe, where he now lines up alongside his (adopted) compatriot Pablo Álvarez.
