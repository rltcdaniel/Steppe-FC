Biography

Agustín Emanuel Pastoriza Cacabelos, known as "Pibe", was born in Argentina but moved with his family to Galicia at just five years old, to Cambados, his mother's hometown. His entire football education is Spanish: he came through Pontevedra and made his La Liga debut with Real Betis in 2014, aged just 18.

His adult career has played out almost entirely in Galician regional football (Pontevedra, Ourense, Arenteiro) and Spain's fourth tier (Cultural Leonesa), with a brief spell in Finland along the way. In January 2026 he signed for Kazakhstan's Aktobe, where he now lines up alongside his (adopted) compatriot Pablo Álvarez.

Current season (FotMob): 1 goal and 1 assist in 654 minutes this season, average FotMob rating of 6.8; 6.9 in his most recent match per Sofascore (Aktobe 2-2 Ulytau). Market value: €280,000. Data as of 23/09/2026.

His FotMob performance graph draws one of the most polarised profiles among the whole Spanish contingent in the region: he's barely involved in ball circulation (23rd percentile for touches, well below his positional average), but the moment he receives it he acts on pure finishing instinct (89th percentile for both shot attempts and goals). It's the statistical signature of the classic inverted winger: he receives already body-oriented towards goal, cuts inside on the diagonal from the right flank, and looks quickly for the low finish or the curled shot rather than delaying the play by combining with the pivot or the inside midfielder. His defensive contribution (8th percentile) is practically nil, something any coach has to accept going in: he's not a winger to ask for tracking back or covering the far side, and his output is maximised in systems with a wing-back or full-back covering behind him. His aerial duels won figure (43rd percentile) is surprisingly high for his frame — left-footed and just 1.77 m per Sofascore — suggesting good anticipation and reading of second balls rather than pure physical dominance. It's also worth noting that Sofascore lists him under Argentine, not Spanish, nationality, despite his entire football education being Spanish — a reminder that databases usually set nationality by country of birth rather than football pathway. His rating curve over the last year (steady between 6.7 and 7.6) confirms a consistent level of performance already in Kazakhstan. His career backs up this reading: 13 goals in 82 games for Arenteiro (2022-2024) and a La Liga debut with Betis at 18 confirm a finishing quality that has never been in question — what's been missing is continuity within a single project, common for high-impact, low defensive-commitment wide players in the lower tiers of Spanish football.
