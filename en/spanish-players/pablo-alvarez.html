Biography

Pablo Álvarez García came through Villarreal's academy and played for Alavés's reserve side before leaving Spain in 2021 to sign for Bulgaria's Cherno More, where he became a regular starter over several seasons.

After spells in Croatia (Rijeka) and Ukraine (Karpaty Lviv), he signed in 2026 for Kazakhstan's Aktobe, one of the league's historic clubs and, along with FK Atyrau, one of only two named directly after their city.
