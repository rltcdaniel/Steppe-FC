Scout's Report

SFCPlaying-style analysis — Steppe FC Scouting

A centre-back or left-back with almost no public statistical trail from his Spanish spell (Balearic and Extremaduran regional football) or his two seasons in Kyrgyzstan — not unusual in leagues where outside statistical coverage is still limited. What can be assessed is his trajectory: he went from Penya Independent to becoming the first Spaniard in the Kyrgyz league, and a year later stepped up to a bigger club, Alga Bishkek, where he remains a starter in the title race. That kind of progression between clubs within the same country is usually a good sign — it typically means his performances on the pitch have convinced other clubs in the league, not just the one that first signed him.

Biography

Trained in Balearic football (Penya Independent, CE Andratx), Munir — also spelled Monir — Abdeselam El Handi played in Spain's regional leagues and had a brief spell at Extremadura's CD Montijo before becoming, in 2025, the first Spanish footballer to play in the Kyrgyz league, signing for FC Talant.

A year later he changed clubs without changing country: he joined Alga Bishkek, one of the capital's historic clubs, where he still plays. He describes the step up with his new club: "it's much more professional, they demand more and push you harder," with "amazing facilities" including a hotel inside the sports complex.

In August 2026 he faced compatriot Ángel Sánchez Baró in a direct Bars-Alga clash his side ended up losing 2-1 in the 95th minute — Alga's first defeat of the entire season. He's about to complete two years in the country.
